TLP Mail Marker

Apply FIRST TLP 2.0 markings to outgoing email in Roundcube Webmail.

Managed by your administrator. Locked settings and hosts are configured by policy and cannot be changed here.

General

The extension only runs on the webmail domains you authorise below. It never sends data anywhere.

Authorised webmail domains

Add the exact hostname of your Roundcube instance (for example mail.example.gov). Access is requested per domain. Nothing is enabled until you approve the browser prompt.

    Classification behaviour

    TLP does not permit wider sharing without the source’s explicit permission. Downgrades are warned about, never performed silently.

    Markings & appearance

    Organisation profiles

    Share approved levels, a default and the missing-classification rule with Thunderbird. Profiles are editable presets; administrator policy takes priority. Website access is authorised separately.
